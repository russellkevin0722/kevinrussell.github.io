Kevin D. Russell

PhD Candidate, Political Science — Emory University

Hello! My name is Kevin, and I am a sixth-year PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science in the Laney Graduate School at Emory University.

My research centers on political participation in the United States, especially among Black communities. I'm interested in how structural and social factors shape who engages in politics and how.

I have received awards from and presented research at meetings of the American Political Science Association, the National Conference of Black Political Scientists, and the Bouchet Graduate Honor Society.

I received a Bachelor of Arts degree from Northwestern University in Political Science with a minor in African American Studies.
