Kevin D. Russell

Kevin is a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science in the Laney Graduate School at Emory University. His research centers on political participation in the United States especially among Black communities. He has received awards and honors from the American Political Science Association, the National Conference of Black Political Scientists, and the Bouchet Graduate Honor Society. Kevin received a Bachelors of Arts degree from Northwestern University in Political Science with a minor in African American Studies.
